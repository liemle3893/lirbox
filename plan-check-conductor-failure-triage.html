Blind spots & unstated assumptions

Unknown-unknowns found beyond the plan's own claims, and tacit assumptions surfaced by interrogation. All but the last are now closed in r3 — kept here because a design is only as trustworthy as the record of what nearly went wrong in it.

  • unknown-unknown
    The try wrap would have silently zeroed a Harbor dimension. phase_order_matches_meta() matches ^phase\('…'\) with re.M — column-0 anchored. Indenting the body inside try {, which is what anyone would write, empties called and returns False. Nothing in the tier-1 or tier-2 nets would have caught it; the failure surfaces only as a tier-3 score quietly dropping. Closed in r3: emit the body unindented, and failure-record asserts column 0.
  • unknown-unknown

The postmortem's kind is exactly the kind of claim this repo elsewhere refuses to take at face value — dodgate-verify.txt states outright that worker self-reports are untrusted. Every other route ends at a human; mechanical did not, which made it the one place a wrong answer is invisible and self-perpetuating. Closed in r3: triage.cjs re-derives mechanical from the error text; unrecognised routes to a question.
  • unknown-unknown

  • unknown-unknown
    The gather protocol quietly crossed two trust boundaries the plan never named. Step 5 sends failure text off the machine; step 4 reads notes whose content may originate anywhere and pipes them toward a prompt that edits code. Closed in r3: web is a launch-time args.web opt-in, external content is capped at 2 KB per source, attributed, and fenced.
  • unknown-unknown

  • unknown-known
    The catch must rethrow the ORIGINAL error. Never stated. A postmortem that dies, returns null, or is itself aborted must not replace the failure it was sent to explain — otherwise the diagnostic layer becomes the thing that hides the diagnosis.
  • unknown-known
    The try must begin after the resume reachability guard. Never stated. A corrupt-phasesDone throw would otherwise burn a postmortem worker classifying garbage, and produce a confident record about a run that never started.
  • unknown-known
    "Every work phase" glosses three distinct dispatch shapes. A phase runs as a level fan-out, as a single worker when the plan holds one item (items.length === 1), or as one serial worker under --no-plan-fanout. A hint reaching only the fan-out path silently does nothing on the other two — the quietest possible failure for a feature whose job is to carry information forward.
